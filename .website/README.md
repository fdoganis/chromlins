# Chromlins

A WebXR AR whack-a-mole game for js13k 2026.

Color spirits, the Chromlins, have stolen the seven colors of the rainbow and captured the unicorn which used to play on it. Find them peeking out of holes and tap them to take the colors back. But beware, if you tap the unicorn, you will both be sad, and you will lose points, as well as colors.

# The Game

- 45 seconds per level
- 7 levels
- Hi Score entry
- a secret bonus level 13! (will you unlock it?)
- exciting music

# Hardware

Best experienced on WebXR compatible headsets with video passthrough (Meta Quest 2 and 3 for example), and your bare hands (see video).

Due to size constraints, the published game only supports a few AR devices, but more might come in the director's cut.

You can also play on your mobile phone:

- Android; make sure to have a recent version of Chrome
- iPhone: open the web page in yourWebXR browser of choice (search the app store for WebXR. I recommend the excellent Hello XR or the OG: Mozilla's XR Viewer)

You can play on your desktop, although definitely not the best experience

- Install Meta WebXR Emulator (IWER), and choose an environment.
